85.6 × 54 mm · CR80 card

PNG and JPG are written at 300 dpi and trim size, 1012 × 638 px, which is what a CR80 card printer expects for an edge-to-edge print. SVG carries the artwork at true size with the bleed, cutting and safety layers. Print sends the card to the browser at 91.6 × 60 mm, the trim plus 3 mm of bleed.

How the card is drawn

The name is hashed to 128 bits, and that number lays out every cell of the field in reading order: quarter circles, split squares, circles, squares and gaps, in the shares the brand guidelines document. One family of colour leads, chosen by the hash or by the visitor's area of interest, and a second appears in about one cell in eight. The initials are written into the field in that second colour, one cell in from the top-left corner, so the cut never touches them.

This is the same generator that draws the business card, the ID card and the luggage tag in the print artifacts, so a mark made here and a mark printed on a student's card from the same name are the same artwork.
